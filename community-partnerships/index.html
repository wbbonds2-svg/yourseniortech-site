Bring a Free Tech Program to Your Community

Senior living communities, libraries, senior centers, and organizations in King and Pierce Counties can host a free Mobile Tech Clinic or workshop.

How do we host a free tech program for seniors? Fill out the form on this page. Senior Tech Foundation will follow up to plan a date, location, and topics. Hosting is free. Hosts provide tables, chairs, power, and Wi-Fi when available.

Programs you can host

  • Mobile Tech Clinics with one-on-one help from trained student mentors
  • Tech Talks and workshops, usually one to two hours
  • Scam and fraud prevention
  • AI for older adults
  • Recurring on-site support so residents have a familiar, trusted presence

Why communities partner with us

  • Residents gain confidence and independence with technology
  • Scam prevention reduces risk and stress for residents, families, and staff
  • Technology questions no longer fall on busy staff
  • Intergenerational connection between residents and student mentors
  • Programs are tailored to each community’s needs
