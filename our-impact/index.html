Our Impact

Real people helped, real hours given, and real scams stopped in King and Pierce Counties.

Impact to date

2,600+older adults served
430+hours of one-on-one tech support
100+community talks and workshops
15+active student mentors

Totals reported by Senior Tech Foundation in its 2026 Mobile Tech Clinic sponsorship materials.

What each number means

  • Older adults served: people reached through one-on-one help, clinics, and group Tech Talks.
  • Hours of one-on-one support: time mentors spent side by side with an individual, recorded in service logs.
  • Community talks and workshops: group sessions on technology, digital safety, scams, and AI.
  • Active student mentors: trained high school and college volunteers currently serving.

How we measure impact

We track impact through scheduled programming, mentor service logs, and community education sessions. Hours are recorded for both group Tech Talks and one-on-one help. People reached is based on attendance at Tech Talks plus tracked individual support. We review and update these numbers regularly as programs grow.
