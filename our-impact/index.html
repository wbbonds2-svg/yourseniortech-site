How We Measure Impact

Your Senior Tech tracks impact through scheduled programming, technician service logs, and community-based education sessions. Hours of service are recorded for both group Tech Talks
and one-on-one technical assistance. Estimated seniors reached is calculated using average Tech Talk attendance combined with tracked individualized support hours. Impact metrics are
reviewed regularly and updated as programs expand to new communities.

Why Organizations Choose Your Senior Tech

Organizations choose Your Senior Tech because we deliver technology support that works and relationships that last.

Our intergenerational model is at the core of everything we do. We intentionally pair older adults with trained students and young adults who bring patience, familiarity with technology, and a genuine willingness to help. For seniors, this feels less like “IT support” and more like learning from someone who takes the time to listen. For communities, it creates meaningful engagement that goes beyond fixing devices.

Read More

This model builds trust quickly. Seniors feel comfortable asking questions. Confidence grows. Technology becomes less intimidating and more usable. At the same time, younger team members develop empathy, communication skills, and real-world experience working with older adults. Both generations benefit, and communities see stronger connections form as a result. 

Organizations also choose Your Senior Tech for our consistency and reliability. Through ongoing community partnerships, we become a familiar and dependable presence rather than a one-time service. Residents know who we are, staff know what to expect, and support feels seamless over time.

Digital safety is another key reason partners work with us. We help seniors recognize scams, protect their personal information, and navigate technology with confidence, reducing risk and stress for residents, families, and staff.

Above all, we are easy to work with. We communicate clearly, adapt to each community’s needs, and deliver thoughtful, dependable service that organizations can trust.

Benefits for Senior Living Communities

Meaningful Intergenerational Engagement

Your Senior Tech’s intergenerational model creates genuine, positive connections between residents and younger team members. Seniors receive patient, respectful support from people who take the time to listen, while students and young adults gain real-world experience and empathy working with older adults. This approach turns tech support into meaningful engagement that enriches community culture.

Improved Resident Satisfaction and Confidence

Residents feel more comfortable asking questions, learning at their own pace, and using technology without fear or embarrassment. As confidence grows, frustration decreases and residents feel more connected to family, friends, and daily life

Increased Appeal to Prospective Residents and Families

Reliable technology support is increasingly important to seniors and their families when choosing a community. Offering dedicated tech support signals that a community is modern, responsive, and resident-focused. Knowing their technology needs will be handled helps prospective residents feel reassured and confident in their decision to move in.

Ongoing, Consistent Support

Through recurring on-site visits, communities benefit from a familiar and dependable presence rather than one-time solutions. Residents know who to turn to for help, trust builds over time, and technology support becomes seamless and reliable.

Enhanced Digital Safety

Scam prevention and digital safety education help protect residents from financial loss, identity theft, and online threats. This proactive approach reduces risk, stress, and potential emergencies for residents, families, and staff.

Reduced Burden on Staff

Technology questions often fall to staff members who already manage full workloads. Your Senior Tech handles these needs directly, allowing staff to focus on care, programming, and operations while knowing residents are receiving reliable support.

Flexible, Community-Specific Programming

Support schedules, focus areas, and educational sessions are tailored to each community’s needs. Programs adapt as resident interests and technology use evolve, ensuring continued relevance and value.

A Trusted Community Partner

Your Senior Tech operates as a professional, reliable partner that communicates clearly, shows up consistently, and understands the unique dynamics of senior living communities.

Our partners

Benefits for Corporate Partners

Partnering with Your Senior Tech allows corporations to create meaningful community impact while aligning with values of access, safety, and intergenerational connection.

Authentic Community Impact

Your Senior Tech works directly with older adults who are navigating an increasingly digital world. Corporate support helps protect seniors from scams, reduce digital exclusion, and improve access to technology in a tangible, measurable way. This is impact that is easy to understand and easy to stand behind.

Alignment with Social Responsibility Goals

Supporting senior technology access, digital safety, and intergenerational programs aligns with corporate priorities around inclusion, education, workforce development, and community well-being. Partnerships offer a clear way to demonstrate commitment to social impact beyond traditional philanthropy.

Intergenerational Workforce Engagement

Our intergenerational model creates opportunities for students and young adults to gain real-world experience, communication skills, and empathy while supporting older adults. Corporate partners can align with programs that foster workforce readiness and cross-generational understanding.

Brand Visibility and Trust

Partnering with Your Senior Tech associates corporate brands with trust, safety, and positive community outcomes. Supporting seniors, particularly around digital safety and scam prevention, builds credibility with families, communities, and local stakeholders

Flexible Partnership Opportunities

Corporate partnerships can support specific programs, communities, or educational initiatives. Whether through sponsorships, program funding, or collaborative initiatives, partnerships are structured to align with corporate goals while meeting real community needs.

Meaningful Impact Without Operational Burden

Your Senior Tech manages program delivery, coordination, and execution. Corporate partners are able to create visible, meaningful impact without taking on additional operational complexity

Sponsorship and Partnership Opportunities

Your Senior Tech partners with organizations that want to create meaningful, visible impact for
older adults through technology, safety, and intergenerational connection.

Rather than offering rigid sponsorship tiers, we work collaboratively with partners to align
support with specific impact areas, communities, or initiatives. This approach allows
partnerships to be flexible, intentional, and responsive to real needs while creating outcomes
partners can clearly understand and stand behind.

Partnerships may be short-term or ongoing and are designed to grow over time as community
needs and organizational goals evolve.
