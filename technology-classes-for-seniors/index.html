Technology Classes & Tech Talks for Seniors

Free group technology classes for older adults on smartphones, email, apps, online safety, scams, and AI.

What are Tech Talks? Free group workshops, usually one to two hours, that teach older adults everyday technology skills and digital safety. Topics include smartphones and tablets, email and messaging, common apps, scam prevention, and AI. Every session includes time for questions.

Topics we teach

  • Smartphone and tablet basics
  • Email, messaging, and video calls
  • Navigating common apps and settings
  • Passwords, passkeys, and account security
  • Scam and fraud prevention
  • Artificial intelligence, explained simply

How sessions work

Sessions can be one-time events or an ongoing series. We tailor each one to the group’s interests and comfort level, and we use real-world examples. Participants can ask questions and talk through real concerns.

Designed for

Senior centers, libraries, retirement communities, housing communities, faith organizations, caregivers, and families in King and Pierce Counties.

Common questions

Is the help really free?

Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s clinics, one-on-one help, and Tech Talks are free for older adults. Our programs are paid for by donations, grants, and sponsors, so there is never a charge to the people we help.

How long are workshops?

Most workshops are one to two hours long and include time for questions.

How can my community host a free clinic or workshop?

Fill out the program request form. We’ll follow up to plan a date, location, and topics.
