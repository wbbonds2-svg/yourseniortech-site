Scam & Fraud Prevention for Seniors

Free scam and fraud prevention workshops that help older adults spot phishing, impersonation, AI voice-cloning, and financial scams.

How can seniors learn to avoid scams? Senior Tech Foundation offers free scam prevention workshops and one-on-one help. Older adults learn to recognize warning signs, pause and verify suspicious calls, texts, and emails, and protect their accounts and personal information.

Topics covered

  • Identity theft and identity protection
  • Current scams and new fraud trends
  • Artificial intelligence, deepfakes, and voice-cloning scams
  • Phishing emails, texts, and fake websites
  • Social media and online marketplace scams
  • Romance and impersonation scams
  • Banking, payment, and financial scams
  • Healthcare, Medicare, and government impersonation scams
  • Data breaches and personal information exposure
  • What to do after a scam or security breach

Practical skills you’ll leave with

  • Recognize common warning signs before responding
  • Pause and verify suspicious calls, texts, emails, and websites
  • Create stronger passwords and turn on multi-factor authentication
  • Check links, senders, and requests before sharing information
  • Adjust privacy and security settings on your own devices
  • Know where to report scams and what to do if an account is compromised

If you think you’ve been scammed

Contact your bank right away, then report it to the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov and to the FBI at ic3.gov. Our mentors can help you change passwords and secure your accounts at a clinic.

Common questions

Is the help really free?

Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s clinics, one-on-one help, and Tech Talks are free for older adults. Our programs are paid for by donations, grants, and sponsors, so there is never a charge to the people we help.

Can a mentor tell me if a message is a scam?

Yes. Bring the device with the message to a clinic and a mentor will go through it with you and explain the warning signs.

How can my community host a free clinic or workshop?

Fill out the program request form. We’ll follow up to plan a date, location, and topics.
