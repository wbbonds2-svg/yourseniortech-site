Mobile Tech Clinics for Seniors

Free pop-up tech clinics where older adults bring any device and get one-on-one help from trained student mentors.

What is a Mobile Tech Clinic? A free, pop-up help session at a senior center, library, or community space. Older adults bring any device and get matched with a trained student mentor for one-on-one help. Clinics usually run two to four hours, with Wi-Fi hotspots and take-home safety guides.

What happens at a clinic

  1. Bring any device. Phones, tablets, laptops, smartwatches, e-readers, and other personal technology.
  2. Get matched one-on-one. A trained high school or college mentor gives patient, side-by-side support.
  3. Connect and solve. Our Wi-Fi hotspots support help with apps, accounts, passwords, photos, email, scams, AI, settings, and more.
  4. Leave more confident. Take home practical guides on scams, AI, online safety, accessibility, and device basics.

Who it’s for

Older adults of any skill level, plus family members and caregivers. Mentor staffing scales to expected attendance, typically up to 10 trained student mentors, with more added for larger turnout.

Where clinics happen

Clinics are held at senior centers, libraries, retirement and housing communities, faith organizations, and other accessible community spaces in King and Pierce Counties. Past clinics include a Technology Help for Seniors pop-up at the Kent Senior Center with the City of Kent in August 2026. See upcoming clinics.

Bring a clinic to your community

Hosting is free for senior communities, libraries, and community organizations. Hosts provide tables, chairs, power, and Wi-Fi when available. Request a clinic. Businesses can also sponsor a clinic to keep it free for participants.

Common questions

Is the help really free?

Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s clinics, one-on-one help, and Tech Talks are free for older adults. Our programs are paid for by donations, grants, and sponsors, so there is never a charge to the people we help.

Do I need an appointment?

Clinic details vary by location. Check the event listing or call 253-617-5848 and we’ll tell you how the next clinic near you works.

What should I bring?

Bring your device, its charger, and any passwords you may need (for example, your Apple ID or Google account). A list of questions helps too. Never share your passwords out loud in a public space; mentors will show you how to type them privately.

How can my community host a free clinic or workshop?

Fill out the program request form. We’ll follow up to plan a date, location, and topics.
