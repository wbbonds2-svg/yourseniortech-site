One-on-One Tech Support

Our One-on-One Tech Support program provides personalized, in-person assistance for seniors who want help with their technology in a comfortable, supportive setting. We take the time to work at each individual’s pace, explaining things clearly and answering questions along the way.

Tech Talks & Digital Safety Education

Our Tech Talks & Digital Safety Education program offers interactive group workshops designed to help seniors better understand and confidently use today’s technology. Topics include everyday tech basics, smartphone and tablet tips, email and messaging, and navigating common apps and settings.

We also cover digital safety and scam prevention, including phishing, fake texts and calls, and emerging risks such as AI- generated scams. Select sessions introduce artificial intelligence in a clear, practical way, focusing on what it is, how it appears in daily life, and how seniors can use it safely and responsibly.

Read More

All Tech Talks are customizable based on each community’s interests, needs, and comfort level with technology. Sessions may be one-time events or part of an ongoing series and always include time for questions and real-world examples. The goal is to help seniors feel informed, confident, and in control when using technology

Community Tech Partnership

The Community Tech Partnership is a recurring, on-site support program created specifically for senior living communities. Through scheduled visits, our team becomes a consistent and familiar presence, providing residents with reliable access to technology help over time.

Read More

This program allows communities to offer ongoing tech support without relying on one-time solutions. Residents benefit from continuity, trust, and growing confidence, while communities benefit from a structured, dependable service that adapts as residents’ needs evolve. Scheduling, frequency, and support focus are customized to align with each community’s goals and resident needs.

Digital Safety & Scam Prevention

Who it helps:
This program is designed for seniors and vulnerable community members who are at risk of online scams and digital fraud.

The problem it solves:
Many older adults face threats via phone, email, and online platforms, which can lead to financial loss, identity theft, and diminished confidence with technology.

What outcomes it creates:
Participants gain practical skills to identify and avoid scams, increased confidence in using devices safely, and documented prevention of real-world fraud attempts.

Senior Device Literacy Program

Who it helps:
Older adults who want to learn how to use smartphones, tablets, and computers effectively.

The problem it solves:
Many seniors struggle with basic device operations, apps, and connectivity, which can cause isolation and frustration.

What outcomes it creates:
Participants achieve greater independence in using digital tools, stay connected with family and services, and build confidence navigating today’s technology-driven world.

Book Now

Wellness Through Technology

Who it helps:
Seniors and adults seeking to improve health, fitness, and mental well-being through digital tools.

The problem it solves:
Limited access to wellness programs and health-tracking resources can reduce quality of life and daily engagement in self-care.

What outcomes it creates:
Participants learn to use fitness apps, meditation programs, and virtual exercises, improving physical and mental wellness while building confidence with technology.
