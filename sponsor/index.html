Sponsor a Mobile Tech Clinic

Help older adults stay connected. Your sponsorship removes cost as a barrier for older adults and creates leadership opportunities for young people.

Two generations, one impact

Older adults gain digital confidence, independence, and protection from scams. Student mentors gain leadership, patience, empathy, and real-world communication skills. Each clinic brings generations together around a practical need.

What your organization receives

  • Sponsorship recognition: your name and logo featured as the clinic sponsor
  • Community visibility: printed and digital materials, social promotion, partner outreach, and event signage
  • On-site presence: a resource or branded table to connect with participants, caregivers, and partners
  • Shareable impact: attendance, approved highlights or photos, and impact language for your marketing
