Privacy Policy

How Senior Tech Foundation handles information you share on this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a form, we receive the information you enter, such as your name, contact details, and message. We use it only to respond to you and to run our programs.

How forms are delivered

Website forms are delivered to our email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service. Donations are processed by Givebutter, which has its own privacy policy.

What we don’t do

We never sell or trade your personal information. We don’t ask for passwords through this website, and mentors never collect or record passwords at clinics.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this website. This data is aggregated and doesn’t identify you personally.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email ashlyn@yourseniortech.com or call 253-617-5848.
