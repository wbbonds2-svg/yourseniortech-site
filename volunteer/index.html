Volunteer as a Student Tech Mentor

High school and college students: help older adults use technology with confidence, and build leadership and communication skills.

What mentors do

Mentors work one-on-one with older adults at Mobile Tech Clinics and help at Tech Talks. You’ll help with phones, tablets, laptops, apps, accounts, and scam questions, explaining each step so participants can do it on their own.

What you gain

  • Leadership, patience, and real-world communication skills
  • Volunteer hours and experience for college and job applications
  • Meaningful connections across generations

Training

All mentors are trained before they work with participants. Training covers patient teaching, privacy and safety, and common device questions.
