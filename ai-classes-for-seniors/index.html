AI & Digital Literacy for Older Adults

Free, plain-language introductions to artificial intelligence for older adults: what AI is, how to use it safely, and how to spot AI-generated scams.

Are there free AI classes for seniors near me? Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s Tech Talks introduce artificial intelligence in plain language: what it is, where it shows up in daily life, how to use it safely and responsibly, and how to spot AI-generated scams like deepfakes and voice cloning.

What you’ll learn

  • What AI is, without the jargon
  • Where you already use AI, like voice assistants, photo apps, and search
  • How to try AI tools safely and protect your privacy
  • How to spot deepfakes and AI voice-cloning scams

How it’s taught

AI is covered in group Tech Talks and during one-on-one help at Mobile Tech Clinics. Participants take home a practical AI and safety guide.

At a glance

  • Cost: Free
  • Who: Older adults, families, and caregivers
  • Where: King & Pierce Counties, WA
  • Questions: 253-617-5848

Common questions

Is the help really free?

Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s clinics, one-on-one help, and Tech Talks are free for older adults. Our programs are paid for by donations, grants, and sponsors, so there is never a charge to the people we help.

Do I need to know anything about AI already?

No. Sessions start from the basics and go at the group’s pace.

How can my community host a free clinic or workshop?

Fill out the program request form. We’ll follow up to plan a date, location, and topics.
