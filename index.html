How to get free help

There is no cost, and you don’t need any tech experience.

  1. Find a clinic or call us

    Check the events page for a Mobile Tech Clinic near you, or call 253-617-5848.

  2. Bring your device

    Bring your phone, tablet, laptop, or other device, its charger, and any questions you have.

  3. Get matched with a mentor

    A trained student mentor sits with you, helps at your pace, and explains each step so you can do it yourself later.

Meet our student mentors

Our mentors are trained high school and college students. Older adults get patient help from someone who takes time to listen. Students build leadership, empathy, and real-world communication skills. Two generations learn from each other at every clinic.

Mentors are trained to explain each step clearly, never rush, and protect privacy. They don’t ask for your passwords, and they show you how to keep your accounts safe.

Questions people ask

Is the help really free?

Yes. Senior Tech Foundation’s clinics, one-on-one help, and Tech Talks are free for older adults. Our programs are paid for by donations, grants, and sponsors, so there is never a charge to the people we help.

Who can get help?

Older adults in King and Pierce Counties, plus the family members and caregivers who help them. You don’t need to know anything about technology to come. Beginners are welcome.

What devices can you help with?

Phones, tablets, laptops, smartwatches, e-readers, and other personal technology. Common requests include apps, accounts, passwords, photos, email, settings, video calls, and checking whether a message is a scam.

Who will be helping me?

Trained high school and college student mentors. They work side by side with you at your pace and explain each step so you can do it again on your own.

Where do programs happen?

At senior centers, libraries, retirement and housing communities, and other community spaces in King and Pierce Counties. See the events page for upcoming clinics, or call us to find the next one near you.
