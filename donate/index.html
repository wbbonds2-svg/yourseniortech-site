Donate to Senior Tech Foundation

Your gift keeps every clinic, workshop, and hour of one-on-one help free for older adults.

What your donation does

A single donation can mean an older adult gets one-on-one help with a device they’ve struggled with for months, finally understands a confusing setting, or recognizes a suspicious text before clicking a link.

Donations also fund Tech Talks and digital safety workshops, take-home guides, Wi-Fi hotspots, and training for our student mentors.
