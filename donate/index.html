Donate to Senior Tech Foundation

Your gift keeps every clinic, workshop, and hour of one-on-one help free for older adults.

What your donation does

A single donation can mean an older adult gets one-on-one help with a device they’ve struggled with for months, finally understands a confusing setting, or recognizes a suspicious text before clicking a link.

Donations also fund Tech Talks and digital safety workshops, take-home guides, Wi-Fi hotspots, and training for our student mentors.

Donate securely on Givebutter

Senior Tech Foundation is a Washington 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 41-3502697. Donations are separate from program registration; you never need to donate to receive help.

Businesses can also sponsor a Mobile Tech Clinic.

How we use funding

We’re committed to transparency. Here’s how funding supports our programs for seniors, students, and communities.

Devices for low-income seniors

Providing smartphones, tablets, and computers to older adults who need them.

Free scam-prevention programs

Workshops and take-home materials that teach seniors to stay safe online.

Community workshops

Local events that improve digital literacy and social connection.

Student mentor stipends

Supporting the student mentors who give hands-on help.

Expanding to underserved areas

Bringing programs to communities with the greatest need.
